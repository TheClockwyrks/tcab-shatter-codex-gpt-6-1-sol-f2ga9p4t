# Shatter

One ship. A restless field of armored rock. Clear each wave by breaking the big bodies into smaller fragments, then hunting down every last piece.

The bright star at the centre is your greatest complication and your best opportunity. Bullets curl through its gravity while rocks fall into looping paths. Aim beside the well and let the pull finish the shot. Your ship stays under your control, carrying its momentum wherever you point the thruster.

Armor takes patience. Watch the cracks spread as your shots chip away, or spend your torpedo to destroy a rock outright. Its guidance finds targets in a narrow cone ahead, and its powered flight ignores the pull. You get one charge every ten seconds.

The star never cleans the field for you: swallowed rocks return from the edges. A hunting saucer brings another threat, and every cleared wave brings more rock. Keep moving, make your shots count, and see how long your three ships last.
